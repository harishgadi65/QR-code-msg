Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information the QR Memory service collects and how it is used. "We," "our," and "the service" refer to QR Memory; "you" refers to anyone who signs in, uploads, or views a memory through it.

What we collect

  • Google account info: When you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address, used to identify who uploaded a given memory.
  • Content you upload: Photos, videos, voice recordings, and text messages you choose to attach to a memory, along with any "from"/"to" names you enter.
  • Access credentials you choose: For the shared order QR code, the phone number and 4-digit PIN you set so the intended receiver can look the memory up later.
  • Basic usage data: When a QR code is scanned and how many times, to enforce optional view limits the uploader sets.

How it's stored

Memory content and metadata are stored in Google Firestore. Uploaded media files are stored in Cloudflare R2 (or, for memories saved before this service migrated, Google Drive).

How it's used

Information you provide is used solely to operate the service: saving your memory, letting the intended recipient view it, and letting the business operator manage and support submissions. We do not sell your information or share it with third parties for advertising.

Retention

Content is retained until the sender or the service operator removes it, or until an optional expiry/view-limit the sender set is reached.
